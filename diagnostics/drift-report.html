What changed. A local cosmetic history emits smoke, sparks and tire tracks from rendered wheel positions. It uses the shared effect shapes and aging rules but never replays effects during movement reconciliation. Charge colors update in the milestone frame. Ownership survives compact snapshots, allowing the browser to suppress its delayed effects while retaining other players' trails.

Checks. Method. Real Chrome/WebGL, actual authenticated WebSockets and compression, FIFO latency queues in both directions, and observations from the view consumed by the renderer. Only the test harness exposes internal game objects; production routes do not.
