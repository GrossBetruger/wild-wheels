# Dark Humor recordings

These recordings are included with the game and served locally. Both are published under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

- `assets/audio/dark-humor-crowd.ogg`: [Evil laugh / 020_evil_laugh.wav](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Evil_laugh.ogg), credited to freesound. The source describes a recording of around 40 people in Ghent, directed by Henk. Original file: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/48/Evil_laugh.ogg
- `assets/audio/dark-humor-solo.flac`: [evil laughter](https://opengameart.org/content/evil-laughter-0), Venn Stone. Original file: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/eviljest.flac

The source files are unmodified. Playback layers the crowd and solo performances, offsets their entrances, applies small playback-rate variations, and positions the foreground voices across stereo. A short fade ends the mix when Dark Humor expires or is disabled.
